# Product specification

## Objective

Build a polished, public personality quiz that playfully connects a user's answers with one of four real-asset archetypes: Listed Real Estate, Listed Infrastructure, Commodities, or Natural Resource Equities. The experience is educational and entertaining—not a suitability assessment, risk questionnaire, recommendation engine, or financial advice.

## Journey

Landing → introduction → eight data-driven questions → calculated result → primary and optional secondary personality → asset-class education → configurable CTA → sharing → retake.

No login, email, personal data, backend, database, or authentication is required. Quiz content is actively being refined and is not final approved marketing material.

## Acceptance baseline

The app must be responsive from 320px upward, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion aware, static-host compatible, content-configurable, deterministic in scoring, and verifiably buildable. Development-only previews expose every result and core component without requiring quiz completion.

# Compliance guardrails

This quiz is educational and entertaining. A personality resemblance is not an investment recommendation, suitability determination, risk assessment, or statement that an asset class is right for a user.

Do not invent product names, fund claims, tickers, performance, yields, expected returns, target allocations, inflation-protection claims, endorsements, or legal language. Do not strengthen, weaken, or rewrite the configured disclaimer without instruction.

## Personal data

The quiz collects name, company, email address and quiz result, so the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 applies. The notice in `src/data/privacyNotice.ts` is compliance-approved legal copy, shown without tick-boxes at compliance's request: do not change it or add consent boxes without instruction, and bump `version` when the notice or form changes. Personal data must never appear in URLs, browser storage, analytics events or logs. The form does not collect marketing consent. See `LEAD_CAPTURE.md` for the setup and launch checklist.

Approved copy belongs in data configuration and should not be casually altered during engineering work. Current placeholder copy must remain visibly marked in source until reviewed.

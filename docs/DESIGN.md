# Design direction

The prototype uses a neutral editorial system rather than an invented corporate identity: warm paper surfaces, ink and mineral tones, crisp system typography, generous whitespace, restrained borders, and one dark evergreen interactive accent. Abstract SVG artwork is replaceable through personality configuration.

The interface should feel premium, professional, playful, and magazine-like—not like a calculator or dashboard. Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, neon, crypto motifs, stock charts, remote fonts, and generic market imagery.

Mobile-first breakpoints support 320, 375, 390, 430, 768, and 1024+ pixel viewports. Motion is brief and supportive, and is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
